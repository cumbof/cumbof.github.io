I'm a Software Engineer and Researcher with a Ph.D. in [Computer Science and Automation Engineering](http://phd.dia.uniroma3.it/), currently a Research Associate at the [Blankenberg Lab](https://www.lerner.ccf.org/computational-medicine/blankenberg/), [Computational Life Sciences](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/research/computational-life-sciences), [Cleveland Clinic Research](https://www.lerner.ccf.org/), Cleveland, Ohio, USA.

My work sits at the intersection of bioinformatics, machine learning, and hyperdimensional computing — building computational tools and methods to tackle open challenges in microbial genomics and biomedical data analysis.

My scientific portfolio — peer-reviewed journal articles, conference proceedings, and pre-print manuscripts — is available on [my Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=DJWJY7EAAAAJ&hl=en). My curriculum vitae can be downloaded [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZinL98Rikno3QENxM8tPPaxQUWQsjcBP_B8xzd2CUG8/export?format=pdf).

## Research Interests

My research interests include but are not limited to:

- __Bioinformatics__: developing computational methods in metagenomics for the discovery and characterization of known and yet-to-be-named microbial species, with a focus on strain-level taxonomy and large-scale metagenomic profiling;

- __Machine Learning__: designing energy- and memory-efficient machine learning methods for analyzing large-scale biomedical datasets;

- __Hyperdimensional Computing__: developing and applying Hyperdimensional Computing (Vector-Symbolic Architectures) to artificial intelligence and bioinformatics problems, including classification and feature selection on high-dimensional metagenomic data;

- __Quantum Computing__: exploring the intersection of quantum computing and Hyperdimensional Computing, and the development of new quantum neuromorphic architectures.
